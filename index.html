How to use the JoyaGoo spreadsheet

Open the matching listing

  1. Find a product here, then search its name on CAFANS.
  2. On the product page, open Buy Link and choose JoyaGoo if it appears. If the link fails, check whether JoyaGoo can open the original seller URL.
  3. Match the seller, colour, size and variant price before placing the order.

Check the item before shipping

  • Compare the warehouse photos with the exact variant you ordered.
  • For tops, request a flat chest-width or length measurement if sizing is unclear. For shoes, check the size label and confirm whether the seller’s chart gives foot or insole length.
  • For patterned pieces, compare a close-up and a full-item photo. Check the seller’s exchange window if you spot a mismatch.

Frequently asked questions

What is a JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

It is a browsable product list for shoppers who want to compare items before using JoyaGoo. This edition uses listings from the CAFANS catalogue.

What does the estimated USD price cover?

It reflects the listed item price converted from Chinese yuan. Domestic delivery, international shipping, service fees and any import charges go into the order budget separately.

Where can I see warehouse QC photos?

Check the product in your agent account after warehouse intake. Use photos attached to your exact item and selected variant when deciding whether to ship.

Does Buy it now place my order?

The button opens CAFANS. Find the item there, choose an available buying option and review the terms and total on the order screen.
